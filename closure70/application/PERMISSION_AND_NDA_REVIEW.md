# Permission/NDA review — questions, not an official agreement

Known restriction: the 2015 participant states that they cannot upload the original dataset. **No actual NDA text or current licence has been obtained.** The following are proposed review questions, not asserted Uken requirements.

1. Who owns/controls the data and may grant current access? Is SFU an archive, processor, research partner or licensee? Does the original agreement permit new independent/commercial research?
2. Who can apply? Must there be a university sponsor or ethics review? What use, staff and compute environment are permitted? Do not claim eligibility before confirmation.
3. Are actual files, field definitions and observation/scaling semantics available? Are transformed revenue and duration still licensed? Does the scope cover zero-revenue users and historical cohorts?
4. Is model training allowed? Can aggregate results, code, fitted model artefacts or benchmark metrics be published? Are there disclosure-review and suppression rules?
5. Is data export allowed or only provider-controlled computation? Which regions, access logs, encryption, retention and deletion verification apply?
6. Does approval exclude re-identification, external joins and onward sharing? How are deletion requests and consent/observation gaps represented?
7. What fees, liability, confidentiality, publication controls and termination obligations apply? Paid consulting does not itself confer third-party data rights.
8. Who signs on behalf of the applicant? The assistant cannot sign, accept terms, send an application, or imply agreement on the user's behalf.

Training admission remains STOP until the actual permission, dataset, money/time definitions, observation scope and mature labels are verified together. Permission to read a paper or public repository is not permission to reuse the underlying private data.
